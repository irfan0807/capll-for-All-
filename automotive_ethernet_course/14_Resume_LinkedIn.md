## 14.2 RESUME WRITING RULES

### The STAR-Bullet Formula

```
Every bullet point = Action Verb + What You Did + Result/Metric

WEAK BULLET:
"Responsible for testing the ECU communication"

STRONG BULLET:
"Validated SOME/IP service interface for 12 ECU nodes across ADAS domain
 controller, identifying 4 service ID mismatches pre-integration, saving
 2 weeks of debugging effort"

ACTION VERBS TO USE:
Validated, Developed, Automated, Identified, Implemented, Designed,
Optimized, Reduced, Increased, Delivered, Analyzed, Debugged, Created,
Established, Led, Mentored, Integrated, Documented

METRICS TO INCLUDE:
• % improvement: "reduced test execution time by 85%"
• Count: "automated 120 test cases"
• Time saved: "saved 2 weeks per release cycle"
• Defect severity: "identified S1 safety defect pre-DVT"
• Coverage: "achieved 98% requirement coverage"
• Scale: "validated across 12 ECUs in ADAS network"
```

## 14.5 INTERVIEW PREPARATION CHECKLIST

### Before the Interview

```
48 HOURS BEFORE:
□ Research company's automotive products (go to their website)
□ Find the interviewer on LinkedIn (note their background)
□ Read the job description 3 times — note every requirement
□ Prepare 3 questions to ask the interviewer
□ Charge laptop, test internet connection (for video interview)

24 HOURS BEFORE:
□ Review Section 15 (crash course cheat sheets)
□ Practice 5 STAR stories out loud (not just in your head)
□ Prepare answer for: "Walk me through your most complex debugging case"
□ Prepare answer for: "Why do you want to join [Company]?"
□ Sleep 8 hours (non-negotiable for technical interviews)

ON THE DAY:
□ Wake up 2 hours before interview
□ Review protocol cheat sheet (15 minutes)
□ Eat a real meal
□ Test audio/video 30 minutes before (for virtual)
□ Have pen and paper ready for diagrams
□ Have GitHub open in a browser tab
```

### Questions to Ask the Interviewer

```
SMART QUESTIONS TO ASK:

Technical:
1. "What protocols does your Ethernet backbone use — 100BASE-T1 only, 
   or are you moving to 1000BASE-T1 for the camera domain?"

2. "Are you using TSN for time-critical ADAS traffic, and if so,
   which specific 802.1 standards — Qbv, Qbu, or CB?"

3. "What is your current automation coverage for Ethernet tests,
   and is there a roadmap to increase it?"

Role-specific:
4. "What does a typical day look like for a validation engineer
   on your team — bench work, code review, meetings?"

5. "What's the biggest validation challenge your team is currently
   facing with the [product name] project?"

Culture/Growth:
6. "What would success look like for this role in the first 6 months?"

AVOID:
"What is the salary?" (wait for them to bring it up)
"How many vacation days?" (sounds like you're not interested in work)
"What does your company do?" (basic research failure)
```
